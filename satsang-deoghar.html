Satsang Ashram, Deoghar

Satsang Ashram in Deoghar is a significant place of devotion for followers of Sri Sri Thakur Anukulchandra. This page provides background and directs visitors to official channels for time-sensitive details.

Verified background

The Deoghar district administration describes Satsang Ashram as a devotional destination associated with Thakur Anukulchandra, and notes its location in the south-west of Deoghar.

Before you travel

Accommodation, opening arrangements, ceremonies, transport and contact details can change. Confirm them directly with the official Satsang organisation before booking travel. Do not rely on an old blog or a search snippet.

Getting oriented

The district page gives broad travel context; official Satsang contact details are the right source for programme-specific questions.

Sources and editorial note

Linked sources support the factual background on this page. This independent site does not claim to be an official Satsang publication; it does not publish unverified sacred text, quotations, centre details or permissions.
