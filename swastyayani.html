What is Swastyayani?

Swastyayani is commonly searched alongside Istavrity and Satsang observances. Accurate guidance requires an authoritative source and, where needed, guidance from an authorised local contact.

Why source-first matters

A practice can be misunderstood when reduced to a short web definition. This page therefore gives no invented steps, schedules, promises or religious claims.

How to research it

Look for the term in an authorised publication, confirm the edition and language, and ask the official organisation for current guidance. Preserve the distinction between a quotation and an editorial explanation.
