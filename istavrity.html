What is Istavrity?

Istavrity is a frequently searched Satsang practice. Because it concerns personal observance, this page avoids prescribing a method or substituting for guidance from an authorised Satsang source.

Start with an authorised explanation

Names and explanations may differ by language and tradition. The current official Satsang contact channel is the appropriate place to ask for an authoritative explanation and practice guidance.
