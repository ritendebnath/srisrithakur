Find a Satsang centre

Use the existing global map to begin a centre search. Centre addresses, contacts, prayer times and events change, so this site does not invent or auto-generate local listings.

Use the global map

The centre map is the site’s directory entry point. A listing should show its verification date and a direct contact method before visitors rely on it.

Verify before visiting

Confirm the current address, time and accessibility requirements with the listed centre or official organisation. Search results and map pins can be out of date.

For centres

Submit changes with a public contact, full location and authorisation to publish. Entries should remain unpublished until reviewed.
