Satyanusaran

Satyanusaran (The Pursuit of Truth) was written in Bengali. The published English edition’s preface dates its composition to 1910 and first printing to 1918. Start with our editorial guide, then use an authorised edition for the text itself.

Finding an edition

Edition, translator, language and permission matter. Link only to a publisher, official organisation, library or rights-holder that makes the edition available. Do not upload or mirror a PDF merely because it appears in search results.

Reading guide

Use the book’s own edition details when citing it. Editorial explainers should clearly label summaries as summaries and should not turn paraphrases into quotations.
