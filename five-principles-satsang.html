Five principles of Satsang

People search for “five principles of Satsang” and “Panchaniti” looking for clear guidance. The principles should be read in their authorised source and context, rather than reproduced from an unattributed social post.

Read the source, not a fragment

Different spellings and short lists online can omit context. Verify the original work, edition, language and translation before relying on a list or sharing it.

For first-time visitors

The official Satsang contact channel can direct readers to current authorised literature or an appropriate centre. This is particularly important when a question concerns personal practice or initiation.
