Sri Sri Thakur and Satsang FAQ

Short, source-linked answers to common questions. Where a question concerns current arrangements or personal spiritual guidance, contact an authorised source directly.

Who was Sri Sri Thakur Anukulchandra?

He was born in Himaitpur, Pabna, on 14 September 1888 and is associated with the Satsang movement. See the biography page and the Deoghar district source for a documented introduction.

Where is Satsang Ashram?

Satsang Ashram is in Deoghar, Jharkhand. Use the official Satsang contact channel for present-day visitor information.

Where can I read Satyanusaran?

Start with the book guide and an authorised edition. Check the edition, language and publisher before downloading or sharing any copy.
