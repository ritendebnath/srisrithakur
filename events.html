Digital Library

His books, discourses and magazines

গ্রন্থাগার

A catalogue of the published works of Sri Sri Thakur Anukulchandra. Every entry shows its language, translations and source.

Banglapedia records 94 books by Sri Sri Thakur, 82 in Bengali and 12 in English, and many have been translated into other Indian languages. This catalogue starts with his best-known works and grows as each entry is checked against a printed edition. The books are published by Satsang Publishing House, Deoghar, and are not hosted on this site. For a copy, ask at your nearest Satsang Vihar.

    About this initiative

    A personal offering, made with gratitude

    এই উদ্যোগ সম্পর্কে

    This website is an independent, non-commercial effort to share the teachings of Sri Sri Thakur Anukulchandra and to help devotees around the world find one another.

    Please note

    Not affiliated with Satsang

    This website is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Satsang, Deoghar, any Satsang Vihar, or any official organisation associated with Sri Sri Thakur. It does not speak on their behalf.

    For official information, initiation, events, or publications, please contact Satsang, Deoghar or your local Satsang Vihar directly.

    Independent and personal

    Started and maintained by an individual devotee as a personal philanthropic project. It is not an official platform.

    Non-commercial

    Nothing here is sold. There are no fees, no paid memberships, and no advertising. No one makes money from this site.

    Source-first

    Teachings are published only with their original text and a cited source. Anything not yet verified is clearly marked.

    Volunteer-run

    Contributors give their time freely. Personal details are used only for the purpose stated and never sold or shared.

    Why this exists

    The teachings and blessings of Sri Sri Thakur have shaped my life in ways I am still discovering. This site is my small way of giving back: a calm, open place where anyone, anywhere, can read his words, learn about his life, and find others who share the same path.

    It is built in a spirit of service. If you notice an error, a missing source, or anything that could cause confusion, please let us know so it can be corrected.

    Riten, founder of this initiative

    How it works

    From source to screen

    Step 1

    Selected from the source

    A teaching is chosen from a published work, with edition and page noted.

    Step 2

    From the English edition

    Excerpts retain the wording of the supplied English PDF. Text extraction spacing has been corrected; no new translation has been added.

    Step 3

    A new excerpt each day

    The 365 excerpts rotate daily at midnight in India. Page references count the PDF cover as page 1.
