Sri Sri Thakur Anukulchandra

Sri Sri Thakur Anukulchandra (1888–1969) is widely known as the founder of the Satsang movement. This introduction is for readers searching for Sri Sri Thakur, Thakur Anukulchandra, Anukul Chandra, or Anukulchandra Chakravarty.

Life at a glance

The Deoghar district administration records his birth on 14 September 1888 in Himaitpur, Pabna—now in Bangladesh—and identifies Satsang Ashram in Deoghar as a place of devotion associated with him. A responsible biography should distinguish documented history from devotional interpretation.

Himaitpur and Deoghar

Satsang began in Himaitpur. The Deoghar Ashram was established after the move to Deoghar in 1946; visitors should use the official organisation’s contact channel for current practical information.
