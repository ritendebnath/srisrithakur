Start with Satyanusaran

His first book, written in 1910, and the usual place to begin. It is short and has been translated into at least 14 languages.

  • সত্যানুসরণ

    Satyanusaran

    Written in his own hand in 1910, when he was 22, to guide a devotee, Atulchandra Bhattacharya. The English translation, The Pursuit of Truth, first appeared in 1950.

    Bengali · also in English, Hindi, Odia, Assamese, Telugu and 9 more

Books of sayings and verse

Most of his books gather short sayings and verses, each a few lines long, grouped by subject.

  • অনুশ্রুতি

    Anushruti

    Teachings in rhyming verse on many subjects of daily life, published in several volumes.

    Bengali · also in Hindi

  • পুণ্যপুঁথি

    Punya Punthi

    Bengali

Conversations and discourses

Devotees wrote down his conversations with visitors and followers as they happened. These volumes record his answers on everyday questions of work, family, society and faith.

  • আলোচনা প্রসঙ্গে

    Alochana Prasange

    Conversations with devotees and visitors, compiled by Prafulla Kumar Das across many volumes.

    Bengali · also in English, Hindi, Odia

  • নানা প্রসঙ্গে

    Nana Prasange

    Conversations on a wide range of subjects, from family and work to society.

    Bengali · also in Odia

  • ইসলাম প্রসঙ্গে

    Islam Prasange

    Discussions on Islam, aimed at easing sectarian conflict and showing the common ground between faiths.

    Bengali · also in Hindi

  • ইষ্ট প্রসঙ্গে

    Ishta Prasange

    Conversations centred on the Ishta, the living Ideal.

    Bengali · also in Hindi, Odia

  • শ্রীশ্রীঠাকুর ও দেশবন্ধু

    Sri Sri Thakur O Deshbandhu Chittaranjan Das

    His conversations with Deshbandhu Chittaranjan Das on Indian independence and social reform.

    Bengali · also in Hindi

Books in English

He also gave sayings directly in English. These are separate from the English translations of his Bengali books.

    His sayings in English on religion, education, society, politics, industry and more, in a multi-volume set.

  • Magna Dicta

  • Discourses

    A short record, in English, of his discussions with eminent visitors.

Where to get his books

Authorised editions are published by Satsang Publishing House, Deoghar, and most Satsang Vihars keep the main titles for devotees. Ask at your nearest centre, which you can find on the Global Map.

On copyright: under Indian law, the books Sri Sri Thakur wrote in his lifetime are expected to enter the public domain on 1 January 2030, sixty years after his passing. Translations, compilations and later editions carry their own rights, so please do not share full scans of a printed edition without the publisher's permission.
