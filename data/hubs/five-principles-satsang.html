The practices most often named

Lists of "five principles" shared online differ from one another. These are the practices devotees name most often, each in plain English.

  • যজন

    Jajan

    Cultivating oneself: spiritual practice, study and living by one's ideals.

  • যাজন

    Yajan

    Sharing what one has received with others, through conduct and conversation.

  • ইষ্টভৃতি

    Ishtabhriti

    A daily offering made with love to the Ideal before one's own needs.

  • স্বস্ত্যয়নী

    Swastyayani

    Vows for wellbeing that link one's own health and character to service of others.

  • সদাচার

    Sadachar

    Right conduct in daily life: in body, in mind and in dealings with others.

Not to be confused with the four ideals

Satsang's welfare work rested on four ideals: education, agriculture, industry and good marriage. Those describe the community's work; the practices above describe a person's daily life. Read about his teachings
