What Swastyayani is

Swastyayani (স্বস্ত্যয়নী) comes from swasti, wellbeing. It is a set of vows for wellbeing that Sri Sri Thakur gave his followers, linking care of one's own body, mind and character to service of the family and the community.

Its idea follows from his teaching of Being and Becoming: a person who keeps themselves healthy, disciplined and of good character is better able to live, to grow and to help others do the same.

Related ideas

  • Sadachar

  • Being and Becoming

    Life is meant to sustain itself and to grow.

Taking the vows

Swastyayani is taken on with guidance from Satsang, and its vows are explained personally. To learn more, ask at your nearest Satsang Vihar, which you can find on the Global Map, or at Satsang, Deoghar.
