What each prayer page gives you

  • The full words of every prayer, in sequence, in the script of your language.
  • The prayer image for each part, to save, print or send to family.
  • A larger text size for reading from a phone during prayer.
  • A copy and WhatsApp button under every prayer.

The morning and evening prayers share the second and third prayers and the closing kirtan; the first prayer is different. Both are sung together in Satsang homes and at Satsang Vihars.

About the languages

The prayers themselves are in Hindi, Sanskrit and Bengali. The English pages give them in English letters, so anyone can sing along. The Hindi pages give the Devanagari text, and the Bengali, Odia and Assamese pages show the same words in those scripts.

If you have a printed prayer book in Bengali, Odia or Assamese and notice a difference in spelling, please send us a photo of the page so we can match it.
