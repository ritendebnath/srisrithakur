His life, year by year

  1. 188814 September

    Born in Himaitpur

    Born in the village of Himaitpur in Pabna district, Bengal, to Shivchandra Chakravarty, a contractor, and Manomohini Devi. It was his mother who first drew him to devotional practice.

  2. School years

    Pabna and Naihati

    Studied at the Pabna Institution up to Class IX, then at Naihati High School in West Bengal.

  3. 1909August

    Marriage to Sarasibala Devi

    Married Sarasibala Devi, remembered by devotees as Sri Sri Boroma.

  4. 1910

    Writes Satyanusaran

    At 22, wrote Satyanusaran in his own hand to guide a devotee, Atulchandra Bhattacharya. It became his best-known book. Read about Satyanusaran

  5. Calcutta

    Training as a physician

    Took a diploma in homeopathy from the Calcutta National Medical School and, as a student, treated people in the city's poorer neighbourhoods. He returned to Himaitpur to practise, treating the mind as carefully as the body.

  6. Kirtan years

    Becoming "Thakur"

    Led a kirtan group in Himaitpur and often went into trance while singing. The words he spoke in those states were later collected as Punya Punthi. From this time people began to call him Thakur.

  7. Himaitpur

    Satsang takes shape

    The community that gathered around him at Himaitpur, open to everyone regardless of caste or creed, became Satsang. His mother was the first president of its council. Over the years it built a school, a charitable dispensary, an engineering workshop, a press and publishing house, the Tapovan school, a chemical works, an art centre, a maternity centre and a farm. See photographs from Himaitpur

  8. 1925

    Satsang registered

    Satsang was registered in Pabna as a public charitable institution. Visitors to Himaitpur over these years included Deshbandhu Chittaranjan Das, Mahatma Gandhi, Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose and A.K. Fazlul Huq.

  9. 193017 November

    Marriage to Sarbamangala Devi

    Married Sarbamangala Devi, the younger sister of his first wife.

  10. 19462 September

    Move to Deoghar

    After 58 years at Himaitpur he moved to Deoghar, then in Bihar and now in Jharkhand, where the new ashram was built. He did not return to Pabna after Partition. Satsang Ashram, Deoghar

  11. 1951

    Satsang re-registered in India

    Satsang was registered again in India, at Deoghar, which remains its headquarters.

  12. 196927 January

    Passing at Deoghar

    Passed away at Deoghar, aged 80. Some sources give the date as 26 January.

  13. 1987

    Postage stamp

    The Government of India issued a commemorative postage stamp in his honour.

  • Sons: Amarendranath (Borda), Vivekranjan (Chorda) and Pracheta Ranjan (Kajalda).
  • Daughter: his youngest daughter, Santwana Devi.

His teaching and writing

Sri Sri Thakur taught that life is meant to be lived and to grow, in body, mind and spirit, and that devotion to a living Ideal is what holds a life together. Satsang's work rested on four ideals: education, agriculture, industry and good marriage.

His words survive in about 94 books, 82 in Bengali and 12 in English, many of them conversations recorded by devotees. The best known are Satyanusaran, Punya Punthi, Anushruti, Chalar Sathi, the Alochana Prasange series and, in English, The Message.
