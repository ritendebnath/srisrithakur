Later years

These photographs carry no printed date or place. Sri Sri Thakur's age in them places them in his later life, most likely at Deoghar, where he lived from 1946 until 1969. Several reached us as colourised copies; they are shown here in black and white. If you know where or when one was taken, please tell us.

About these photographs

Thirteen of these photographs come from printed books, via the srisrithakuranukulchandra.com album, and were taken before 1958. Their dates, places and names come from the captions printed with them; where no date is printed, the photograph is from Sri Sri Thakur's years at Himaitpur, before his move to Deoghar on 2 September 1946.

The other fourteen reached us as copies, with no caption. Their descriptions are ours and say only what each photograph shows, and the era is judged from Sri Sri Thakur's age in it. The line under each one says where the copy came from. Colourised copies are shown in black and white, and where a copy carries a website's watermark, we have left it in place.

All are reproduced for devotional, non-commercial use. If you hold the rights to any of them, know more about one, or want a photograph corrected or removed, please contact us.
