Six ideas at the centre of his teaching

These words come up again and again in his books and in Satsang life. Each card gives a short explanation in plain English; the books are where to read them in his own words.

  • বাঁচা-বাড়া

    Being and Becoming

    Life is meant to sustain itself and to grow. Every habit, choice and practice can be judged by one question: does it help me, and those around me, to live and to grow?

  • ইষ্টভৃতি

    Ishtabhriti (Istavrity)

    A daily offering made with love to the Ideal before attending to one's own needs. It is practised as a discipline of devotion, every day.

  • যজন

    Jajan

    Cultivating oneself: spiritual practice, study and living by one's ideals, so that the Ideal takes shape in one's own character.

  • যাজন

    Yajan

    Sharing with others what one has received, through one's conduct and conversation, so that they too can live and grow.

  • স্বস্ত্যয়নী

    Swastyayani

    A set of vows for wellbeing that ties care of one's own health and character to service of the family and the community.

  • সদাচার

    Sadachar

    Right conduct in daily life: in body, in mind, and in one's dealings with other people.

The Ideal at the centre

Sri Sri Thakur taught that a life held together by love for a living Ideal, the Ishta, finds its direction: work, family, study and service all fall into place around it. Ishtabhriti, Jajan and Yajan are the daily ways of keeping that love active, and Swastyayani and Sadachar keep the body and conduct fit for it.

He spoke to people of every faith and background, and the community he founded at Himaitpur was open to all regardless of caste or creed.

What he spoke about

His sayings and conversations cover almost every part of life. The subjects readers return to most:

  • Spiritual life and devotion
  • Right living and daily conduct
  • Work, duty and earning a living
  • Family, marriage and raising children
  • Character and self-discipline
  • Love and service of others
  • Health and wellbeing
  • Education and society
