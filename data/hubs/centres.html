How the listings work

  • Each pin shows how precise it is: taken from the centre's own Google Maps listing, on the building, at the street, or only at the town.
  • Each listing names its source, so you can check it.
  • Private homes are never published as centres.
  • Timings and contact numbers change, so please confirm with the centre before you travel.
