History

Sri Sri Thakur moved from Himaitpur in Pabna to Deoghar on 2 September 1946, and a new ashram grew up around him here. He lived in Deoghar for the rest of his life, and Satsang was registered again in India, at Deoghar, in 1951. It remains the headquarters of Satsang and the place devotees from India, Bangladesh and abroad come to on pilgrimage.

In the 1950s and 1960s he received visitors here from every walk of life: ministers, writers, scientists, diplomats and thousands of ordinary devotees. Places associated with him at the ashram include Boral, where he spent much of his time, and Bibek-Bitan. See photographs from Deoghar

Before you visit

  • Large numbers of devotees gather for the festivals, especially for Sri Sri Thakur's birth anniversary, which falls in August or September by the Bengali calendar. Plan travel and stays early for those dates.
  • Arrangements for stay, prasad and programmes are made by Satsang, Deoghar. Confirm them with Satsang directly before you travel.
  • To join the morning or evening prayer, it helps to know the words beforehand. Read the prayers
