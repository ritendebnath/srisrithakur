Photos and historical images

Searches for “Sri Sri Thakur photo”, “Thakur Anukulchandra image” and wallpapers should lead to images with provenance—not copied material with an invented date or caption.

What a responsible archive needs

Each image should identify its owner or collection, creator where known, approximate date, place, caption source, rights status and any restrictions on reuse.

Before sharing

Do not assume an image found through Google is free to use. Ask the owning archive or organisation for permission, retain attribution, and do not crop out an existing credit.

Contribute carefully

If you hold an image, preserve the original scan and its reverse-side notes. Submit a copy with provenance for review; publication should wait until rights and description are verified.
