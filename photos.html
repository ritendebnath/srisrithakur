Rare photographs of Sri Sri Thakur Anukulchandra

Thirteen photographs from 1922 to 1957: Sri Sri Thakur with his mother, father, wife, sons and daughter at Himaitpur and Puri, and in Deoghar after 1946. Each caption gives the place, the date where it is known and where the photograph comes from.

Himaitpur and Puri, before 1946

Deoghar, 1951 to 1957

About these photographs

All thirteen were taken before 1958 and are reproduced from published sources for devotional, non-commercial use. Dates, places and names come from the captions printed with them. Where no date is printed, the photograph is from Sri Sri Thakur's years at Himaitpur, before his move to Deoghar on 2 September 1946. If you hold the rights to any of them, know more about one, or want a photograph corrected or removed, please contact us.

Before sharing

Do not assume an image found through Google is free to use. Ask the owning archive or organisation for permission, keep the attribution, and do not crop out an existing credit.

Contribute a photograph

If you hold a photograph, keep the original print and any notes on its reverse. Send a scan with where and when it was taken and who took it or kept it. It is published only after its rights and description are checked.
